## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners in the North Okanagan (working assumption: Vernon, Coldstream and surrounding area; see Operating Context), usually referred by a friend or neighbour. They want someone reliable and tidy for jobs around the house, often general repairs, lighting, a panel upgrade, a basement suite or a hot tub hookup. Mostly on phones. They are not comparing ten electricians; they are confirming the one they were told about.

## Product Purpose

The website for TMF Electric, a one-person residential electrical business run by Taylor. Most work arrives by referral and the business is fairly busy, so the site's first job is credibility: it is where people check Taylor out after a neighbour recommends him. It must confirm fast that he is licensed, local, tidy and easy to reach.

Leads are second. New enquiries are welcome, especially the residential jobs Taylor wants more of (general repairs and service calls, lighting and fixtures, panel upgrades, renovations and basement suites, shops and outbuildings, hot tub hookups), so contact must be easy: a simple form plus call or text. No pushy lead-gen tactics.

Success: a referred homeowner lands, is reassured within seconds, and reaches Taylor in one tap.

## Positioning

A one-person shop by choice, run by Taylor himself, so the person who is recommended is the person who shows up. Backed by a 5.0 Google rating from 26 reviews; customers describe him as punctual, tidy, honest, skilled, to code and fairly priced. A larger contractor cannot truthfully claim the owner does the work himself.

## Product Principles

1. Confirm, don't convince: the visitor already trusts the referral, so the site's job is to verify and reassure quickly, not sell.
2. Phone first: the primary visitor is on a phone, so the path to call or text must always be one tap away.
3. Taylor is the product: a one-person shop run by Taylor is the differentiator, so the site should feel personal and local, not corporate.
4. Show real work: only real client photos and verified facts; no stock imagery, invented credentials, or quotes the client has not approved before launch.
5. Easy, never pushy: make contact effortless and keep lead-gen tactics out.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established. Default to solid contrast, large tap targets and readable text sizes, since the audience is mostly on phones and includes homeowners of all ages.
